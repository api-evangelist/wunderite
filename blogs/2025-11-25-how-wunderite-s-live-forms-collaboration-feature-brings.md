Wunderite’s new Live Forms Collaboration feature lets agents, clients, and team members work on the same form simultaneously — no more version confusion or email back-and-forth. See who’s active, follow collaborator views in real time, and prevent edit conflicts with automatic field locking. This update streamlines submissions, reduces errors, and helps agencies complete forms faster and more accurately.
